# server: rules

The root `CLAUDE.md` applies here too.

- The campaign in `docs/BRIEF.md` takes accounts and PocketBase out of here
  (DECISIONS 87, 89): add nothing to `internal/cold` or
  `migrations`. The lines below describe the tree until then.
- PocketBase is pre-1.0: the exact version is pinned (0.40.4, which needs Go
  1.27; the toolchain fetches itself) and upgraded on purpose, the why in
  DECISIONS.
- `internal/world` is the core and imports no PocketBase type. `internal/cold`
  is all the glue. `migrations` are Go, applied on serve. Settings come from
  the env and are reapplied on start.
- The hot plane never touches PocketBase: permissions are cached in memory and
  invalidated by hooks.
- `internal/protocol` is generated by `bun run proto` and committed. Never
  edited by hand.
