# 108. The core is what a world is made of and how it behaves, as systems, and a plugin speaks to them (decided)

Logged 2026-10-04.

The core holds what a world is made of and how it behaves: the ground, the
cells, the bodies that move in it, their physics. Each is a system: an owner
(93) with its words, ops, questions and events. A plugin is what is done
with them, a tool, a rule or a panel. It speaks to systems in those words and
holds no substance of the world.

**Why.** Marlus: building is tooling that controls voxels. Vehicles,
spaceships and things a body carries are to come, and much of it, physics
included, is the core's. Plugins speak to systems.

**The test.** Two questions say which side a thing is on. With the plugin
off, must what it made still stand and behave? A plugin that is off is
unmounted (106), so that state is the core's. Do two plugins touch it? A
plugin imports the core and never another (93), so what they share is the
core's. A vehicle rests on cells, a thing carried is a body, and physics
crosses all of them: none is one plugin's. A line of chat answers no to both,
and chat owns it.

**What it changes in 88.** 88 drew the core as what a world cannot be walked
without, and everything else as a plugin. The line is now what a world is
made of. The core is larger, and it says nothing of a hand: which tool, which
key, who may, at what price. Those are plugins, and that silence is what
keeps much in the core from becoming everything in the core.

**What stands.** A system is cut when a plugin asks for it and never drawn
ahead (65, 88): the cells by building (106), bodies by the first vehicle. A
system is Rust, written once, in the client and in the server's module (97,
99): the Go server holds no feature and no system. A plugin speaking to a
system is 93's three conversations, and asks for no new mechanism.

**What it costs.** An experiment is a new tool over the same substance. A new
substance, another shape of cell, a ground that is sculpted, is a change to
the core.

**Left open.** Whether bodies are kept as an ECS, and who simulates a body,
its own client or the server (OPEN).

**Rejected:** the core as small as the sphere (88), where what a plugin made
leaves with it; a plugin of bodies or of physics that other plugins import
(93); a system that knows a tool, a key or a permission.
