# A sound is a part with a place and nothing else

**Decision:** A sound placed in the world is a shape of part, Shape::Sound: an MP3, M4A or OGG file played where it stands, looping, heard within the part's reach. It has a place and nothing else: its handles only move it, the panel shows no turn, size or solidity, nothing is drawn of it but its gizmo, and the list of what is near finds it. Any part may carry a name for that list.

**Why:** A sound is placed, moved, picked, removed and taken back like any other thing: making it a part gives it all of that, the one op log and the one undo, for the cost of one shape. Turn and size mean nothing to a point that is heard, so the panel does not offer them. An invisible thing needs a way back to it: a name in the list.

**Rejected:** A sound on the volume's record, heard inside it: that is an ambience, a different thing, and it waits on the volume. A visible speaker model: the sound is the thing, and a marker would be one more thing to hide. A sound as a knob of a picture: a picture already carries its own sound; a sound alone stands where it is placed.
