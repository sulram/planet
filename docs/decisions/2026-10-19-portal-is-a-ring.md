# A portal is a lit ring that leads one way to a landmark

**Decision:** A portal is a shape of part, Portal(to): a standing ring two metres across, its rim in the colour in hand and lit with its own light whatever the finish, a lamp, round a dark pane, leading to a landmark by the name the world gave it; None leads nowhere. A body that walks through the pane, feet to head meeting the circle, is taken through the one go_to to stand on the star, facing the way its point toward +y points. A portal is one way: the way back is a second portal placed by hand at the destination.

**Why:** The landmark is the address and the portal the door: nothing to type, no pair to keep in step, and a landmark removed leaves a portal that leads nowhere. A ring reads as a portal from any side and has no post to walk into; lit, it is found at night as the star is. One way keeps the shape small: a door that knows its pair is two parts kept in step, and a pair is already two portals and two stars placed by hand.

**Rejected:** A doorway, posts and a lintel: a shape with a front, tried first and seen in the world. A destination by text name: two landmarks may share one, and a rename would break the door. A button that places the way back in one gesture: a pair kept in step, for a thing two placements already give. A cooldown by the clock after a leap: the crossing arms itself once the body stands clear of every pane instead, with no clock in a plugin's turn.
