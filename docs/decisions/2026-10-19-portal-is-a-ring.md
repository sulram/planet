# A portal is a lit ring that leads one way to a landmark

**Decision:** A portal is a shape of part, Portal(to): a standing ring two metres across, its rim in the colour in hand, as wide as the hand says, none at all up to the middle, and lit with its own light whatever the finish, a lamp, round a dark pane, leading to a landmark by the name the world gave it; None leads nowhere. A body that walks through the pane, feet to head meeting the circle, is taken through the one go_to to stand on the star, facing the way its point toward +y points. A portal is one way: the way back is a second portal placed by hand at the destination. Its pane is a window on the world past it: the view's camera carried from the ring's frame to the star's, drawn first at a quarter of the view's height, and sampled by the pane where it stands on the screen, the view's own projection kept, so the window has parallax.

**Why:** The landmark is the address and the portal the door: nothing to type, no pair to keep in step, and a landmark removed leaves a portal that leads nowhere. A ring reads as a portal from any side and has no post to walk into; lit, it is found at night as the star is. One way keeps the shape small: a door that knows its pair is two parts kept in step, and a pair is already two portals and two stars placed by hand.

**Rejected:** A doorway, posts and a lintel: a shape with a front, tried first and seen in the world. A destination by text name: two landmarks may share one, and a rename would break the door. A button that places the way back in one gesture: a pair kept in step, for a thing two placements already give. A cooldown by the clock after a leap: the crossing arms itself once the body stands clear of every pane instead, with no clock in a plugin's turn. A still picture in the pane, taken once at the landmark: no parallax, and a view that does not move is a poster, not a door. The view drawn into a picture and tone mapped twice: the one tone map runs once, over the view with the pane in it.
