# Travelling inside the world goes through the passage, and an arrival is a cut under the veil

**Decision:** A place chosen inside the world, a line of the chart, a place shared in chat, an agent's choice, is reached by `Command::Travel`: the passage a portal takes a body by, with no portal, the picture drawn into the body's waist, held while the ground at the place comes, and bursting out of it there. `Command::GoTo` is the arrival, at once: the page opened at an address, a link pasted into its bar, a body born at home, the `--at` of a render. The page veils an arrival and never a travel. A person's click and an agent send the same `travel` (94).

**Why:** The passage is the best transition the world has: a place chosen inside the world comes as the world moving the body, the avatar charged and back, under a second when the ground is near. An arrival comes from outside the world, with nothing on the screen to carry through, so the veil is its wait. Two verbs and not a flag on `GoTo`: they ask different things of a front end, a veil or none, and an agent reads the way it goes in the verb it sends. The waist and not the middle of the view: one point from the pull to the burst, the body sitting in the hole.

**Rejected:** A flag on `GoTo`: a way of going hidden in a field the verb does not say. The middle of the view as the travel's point, tried in the world: the hole sits over the chest and hops down to the waist as the body goes through. A veil over a travel: it hides the passage a portal shows.
