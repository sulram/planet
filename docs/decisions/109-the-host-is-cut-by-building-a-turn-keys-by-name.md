# 109. The host is cut by building: a turn, keys by name, the pointer and the words of the cells (decided)

Logged 2026-10-04.

Building's client half leaves the core for `plugins/build` (106), and what it
asked of the host is cut as it asked: a turn in each frame, its keys asked
for by name, the pointer, and the words of two systems, the cells and the
body. The plugin holds a tool, a stroke and a platform being read. It holds
no cell.

**What a plugin is given.** A turn after the body moved and before the
picture is made, with the eye, the pointer and those of its keys that are
held. Work it spreads over turns it says it owes, and does at once for a
picture that must show it. When its world switches it off or is gone it
rests: it puts down what it had in hand, and the host takes the pointer back.

**Keys are data.** A plugin lists the keys it asks for: a name, the key of a
keyboard as the web names it, the chord held with it, and whether it is read
held or heard once. A shell asks the client whether a key is asked for and
hands it over by that name, so a new plugin's key reaches it with no line
written in a shell, and a headset binds the names to what it has. The core's
own keys stay the enum the shells bind.

**The pointer is lent.** One plugin has it at a time: the pointer stays free
to aim and its button is that plugin's. While it has it, the plugin may let
the body fly through cells. A builder flies through what they build, and
that is building's choice, never a law of the cells.

**The words of the cells.** Read freely: whether a volume holds a cell, what
a cell is, where a corner of one is in the world, a line of sight and what it
meets, what there is to take back. Asked as ops, through the host, which
asks first whether this session may: open the volume of a plot, apply
gestures as one change, show the ghost of a gesture, take a change back, put
it back. A platform is many gestures and one change. Of the body: where its
feet are, and a floor to be lifted onto.

**Why no camera, no tool and no bare address in them.** Marlus: vehicles are
coming, and the cut must not wall them out. A stroke is one way to a gesture.
A builder aboard a vehicle, a vehicle laying track and an agent with no
renderer ask for the same gestures from somewhere else. So a line of sight
is a point and a direction and never an eye, and the cells know of no hand.
Where cells stand is a seat, a union with one member today, a sector of the
planet: a room aboard a ship that moves is the next, and a bare sector in
every word would be taken out of each one then. The plugin asks the cells
where a corner is and does no sum of the sphere itself.

**With no world.** A headless picture and a bench have no world to say what
is on (104), so the client stands in for one where a tool asks. A person's
client hears it from a world alone. Building's world half offers the world no
op: the gesture is the core's, and goes on the socket next (107).

**Rejected:** building's keys as an enum of the core, one more variant for
each plugin's key; whether a hand is at work kept by the cells, a tool's noun
in what a vehicle's collision reuses; the platform kept in `voxel`, a kind of
construction in the core; the client as one struct of systems a host borrows
whole, which moves every field of the client for a name; a welcome fed by
hand to a bench, a link nobody answers and that closes itself in ten seconds.
